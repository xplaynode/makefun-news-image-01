# News of Friday 13 November 2026 — research notes for news-image-01

Researched 2026-11-14 (WITA) from that day's reporting: BBC World and Al Jazeera

## The day's biggest stories (all first reported / happened on 13 Nov 2026)

1. **Senate summons ex-Nvidia CEO** — Jensen Huang, who resigned as Nvidia's
   chief executive on Thursday amid reports of an affair with a woman alleged
   to be a Chinese intelligence agent, was summoned to testify before the US
   Senate Banking Committee; Commerce Secretary Lutnick was summoned too.
   Nvidia shares fell hard (about 14% in one day's reporting). Politics +
   tech + markets — the day's lead story. (BBC, Al Jazeera)
2. **Zelensky meets US envoys; Europe joins the talks** — US envoys Witkoff
   and Kushner met Putin in Moscow, then briefed Zelensky in Dublin; European
   representatives will join the next round of Russia–Ukraine peace talks,
   which Zelensky welcomed. (BBC, Al Jazeera, France 24)
3. **Drone hits Russian warship in the Gulf of Finland** — Finland's military
   detected a strike on a Russian Buyan-M corvette in the Gulf of Finland;
   Ukraine's security service claimed the attack, reported as the first on a
   Russian vessel in open Baltic waters. (BBC, Al Jazeera via Yle)
4. **Iran claims strike on US radar in Bahrain** — the IRGC said it targeted a
   US early-warning radar in Bahrain in retaliation for a strike on a telecom
   tower in Sirik, Iran; CENTCOM denied damage and said it intercepted two
   Iranian drones; a tanker hit a mine in the Strait of Hormuz in the same
   flare-up. A CLAIM, so the image labels it "IRAN CLAIMS…". (Al Jazeera, Reuters)
5. **FBI: North Korea's $400m crypto hack** — the FBI reported North Korean
   hackers stole about $400m in crypto using fake video calls. (BBC)
6. **Microsoft global outage** — a worldwide Microsoft 365 / Outlook / Teams
   outage rolled through the day; Microsoft said it was not a cyberattack,
   apologised, and reported broad recovery by the evening. The day's big tech
   story, and one the house images (drawn ~09:30Z) could not cover. (BBC)
7. **US record deportations** — the US reported a record 483,200 deportations
   in 2026. (Al Jazeera)
8. **Kane breaks England's caps record** — Harry Kane won his 126th England
   cap against Croatia in the Nations League quarter-finals, breaking the
   national appearance record. The day's sports story. (Al Jazeera)

Also reported that day (depth, not pictured): Putin "ups demands" for peace
(BBC analysis); Houthi claim of an attack on Saudi Arabia's East–West oil
pipeline; Pope Leo XIV meeting abuse survivors in Peru; Trump threatening to
double tariffs on Mexico over migrant caravans; an Israeli strike on Nasser
hospital in Khan Younis; Pakistan army raids near the Afghan border.
